# Fintech-product-analytics-
📊 **Project Overview**

An end-to-end fintech analytics project focused on understanding customer activity, product performance, acquisition, and transaction behavior.

The project uses SQL, Power BI, and DAX to transform transactional data into actionable business insights that can support product, growth, and customer engagement decisions.

🎯 **Business Objective**
The objective of this analysis was to:

- Evaluate product performance and customer engagement
- Analyze customer acquisition and active-user trends
- Understand transaction activity and behavioral patterns
- Identify changes affecting product adoption and platform growth
- Provide data-driven recommendations for business decision-making

🛠️ **Tools & Technologies**
- SQL — Data extraction, transformation, and analysis
- Power BI — Interactive dashboards and visualization
- DAX — KPI calculations and business metrics

📈 **Key Business Insights**
1. **Customer acquisition weakened significantly**

New customer acquisition peaked at approximately 1.5K customers in July 2025, but declined steadily afterward, reaching only 100–200 new customers per month by May–June 2026.

This indicates a significant slowdown in customer acquisition and a potential constraint to future business growth.

2. **Active customers declined by approximately 63%**

Active customers peaked at approximately 2.7K in July 2025 before declining to around 1K by June 2026, representing an estimated 63% decline.

This indicates that the business is facing both an acquisition challenge and a significant decline in customer activity.

3. **Existing customers represent the majority of the customer base**

Customer segmentation shows approximately 10K existing customers (55.6%) compared with 8K new customers (44.4%).

With more than half of the customer base consisting of existing customers, retention and continued engagement are critical to sustaining the customer base.

4. **Customer transaction frequency is concentrated among lower-frequency users**

The largest customer segments consist of users making 1 transaction and 2–5 transactions, while considerably fewer customers make 11–20 or 20+ transactions.

This presents an opportunity to increase engagement by moving lower-frequency users toward more frequent usage.

5. **Product performance is highly concentrated**

Card Sales is a major contributor to transaction activity and value, while product-level success rates vary considerably across products.

This suggests that the business relies heavily on certain products for transaction activity while other products have opportunities for performance improvement.




💡 **Business Recommendations**
1. **Strengthen customer acquisition**

Investigate the decline in acquisition across marketing channels, campaigns, referrals, product performance, and geographic markets. Identify the channels generating the highest-quality customers and prioritize investment in those channels.

2. **Prioritize customer retention and reactivation**

Identify inactive customers and analyze their last transaction, product usage, and historical activity. Develop targeted reactivation campaigns and monitor monthly active users and retention rates as core KPIs.

3. **Increase customer transaction frequency**

Create a customer progression strategy:

1 transaction → 2–5 transactions → 6–10+ transactions

Use repeat-use incentives, personalized product recommendations, loyalty programmes, targeted notifications, and cross-selling to increase transaction frequency and customer lifetime value.

4. **Optimize product performance**

Evaluate products using a combination of transaction volume, transaction value, and success rate. Protect high-volume/high-value products while investigating the causes of low success rates in underperforming products.

5. **Investigate payment failures**

Break unsuccessful transactions down by product, country, payment gateway, error/decline reason, and time/shift to identify the specific causes of failed transactions and prioritize corrective actions.

6. **Develop value-based customer segmentation**

Segment customers according to transaction frequency and transaction value to identify high-value, frequent, high-value-but-inactive, and low-frequency customers.

Use these segments to prioritize retention, cross-selling, and reactivation initiatives.

7. **Maintain payment-processing efficiency**

Continue monitoring processing time as a recurring operational KPI, with breakdowns by payment gateway, product, country, shift, and transaction status.

Maintaining processing times below 3 minutes can help preserve operational efficiency and reduce customer friction.

8. **Reassess the Airtime Conversion product**

Investigate the relationship between the removal of Airtime Conversion and subsequent changes in customer acquisition and active-user activity. If the product was a significant contributor to engagement, consider restoring it or introducing an alternative that addresses the same customer need.
